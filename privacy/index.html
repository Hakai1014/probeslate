Privacy

Privacy at ProbeSlate

The current microphone diagnostic processes microphone audio locally in your browser.

Microphone data

The Microphone Buzz & Hum Test requests browser microphone permission only after you start a test. Its current implementation does not upload or store microphone audio, and it does not send the measured spectrum to a ProbeSlate server.

Your browser or operating system may still display its normal microphone-use indicator while a test or optional same-session comparison is active.

Website analytics

ProbeSlate currently uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure page views and page-load performance. Cloudflare describes this service as privacy-first and cookie-free, and states that it does not collect or use visitors' personal data. The analytics service does not receive microphone audio or the spectrum measured by the microphone diagnostic. You can read Cloudflare's Web Analytics documentation for more information.

Advertising and other services

ProbeSlate does not currently run advertising scripts. This policy will be updated before advertising or other third-party services that require additional disclosures are enabled.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the contact information published on the Contact page.
